Signal Quality Gates

What must be true before a challenge signal
Market Structure
REQUIRED

Higher-high / lower-low structure, support/resistance and invalidation must agree with the direction.

Momentum + Volume
REQUIRED

Momentum, volume and candle confirmation are used to avoid weak entries and late chasing.

Risk Gate
REQUIRED

Invalid SL, poor R:R, excessive exposure or daily-loss protection blocks the setup.

Derivatives
CHECK

Liquidity, liquidation and derivatives context should support the setup before execution.

News / Event Risk
CHECK

High-impact news should be treated as a risk filter. No trade is forced just because a signal exists.
