Why a Signal Can Enter

NO-TRADE FIRST
Every challenge trade should pass:
  • High-confidence RR Trader signal.
  • Multi-timeframe structure confirmation.
  • Valid entry and invalidation/SL.
  • Acceptable reward-to-risk.
  • Liquidity / momentum / market context checks.
  • Daily loss and exposure guard.
  • News/context review before publishing when available.

Transparency

COMMUNITY
Post should explain the decision.

The challenge post layer will show the signal, confidence, key reasons, entry, TP levels, SL, risk %, leverage cap and current challenge balance. It will not turn a weak setup into a trade just to create content.

Confidence/reason fields are produced by the live RR Trader signal engine. Existing accountability results do not currently persist those fields, so this page never invents them.
